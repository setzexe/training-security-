# Mobile Device Vulnerabilities

Mobile device security is often hard to control. There are a lot more protocols and procedures in place since these handle alot more than the average desktop computer, especially sensitive data. Unfortunately, these phones are small and coded in ways to make these problems sometimes invisible. Phones are usually also always in motion, you might never even know where the attack is. 

Also just the fact that phones are typically **always connected to the internet**. That is already a layer of security.

## Jailbreaking / Rooting

Jailbreaking for iOS, and Rooting for Android, is the concept of replacing features or the entire OS of a device and replacing it with customware to bypass security features. This term is used in gaming and works the same; you jailbreak a device to bypass security features.. to play games on a device that usually would not allow it due to said security features.

## Sideloading

This is the phone version of apps having malware. These might have malicious code, they're approved on the app store or play store but secretly they might have a trojan. This can also directly lead to jailbreaking.
