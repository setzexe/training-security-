# Capacity Planning

This is about looking ahead so that infrastructure or services might not fall under if real world demand were to spike. The supply should consistently match the demand.

Too much demand and servers with have slowdowns and outages. Too much supply and you're paying too much. A balanced approach will be required.

## People

Some services will never human intervention, maybe through technological services or call center support.

Too few staff is not ideal for demand. Although time consuming, it is ideal to get a proper amount. Too many employees could be a help, but costly.

## Technology

Not all services can easily grow and shrink. Ideally, scalable technologies are preferred. With web servers, this could be load balancing through multiple web servers. With databases, multiple SQL servers could be clustered. Cloud services are also huge because you can typically scale this easily with money because a huge company like Amazon handles this.

## Infrastructure

Infrastructure is important as well. How is a network or system going to be set up? What physical devices will be configured and installed? Will cloud based devices be used, since they are easier to deploy?

The underlying framework, like application and network servers, CPU, storage, OS, etc are importart to consider as well.
