# Disruption and Deception

While you are mostly trying to keep attackers away from your system, you can use your cyber knowledge to decept and disrupt your attackers too. 

## Honeypots

This is the concept of blocking their attack but keeping them in the system in a way that you can see exactly how they are attacking you.

In most cases, it's probably a script / machine. But it is good recon nonetheless.

These honeypots are usually created as virtual worlds with the sole intent of luring attackers. It may look significant, but it is not. You can often find stuff like this open source online.

These are usually part of a whole system of virtual worlds used to deceive attackers called **honeynets**. This is a lot more believable than just one isolated honeypot.

These are often filled with information that is meant to attract attackers. It could look vital or shiny. Perhaps a random folder with files like passwords.txt. The list is fake, but the attacker might use this fake list and waste time. 

By this logic, these fake files could also be **boobytraps**. If they are accessed, that means an attacker is actively attempting to breach. You can also include ways to track that data. Trackers, fake emails, so on and so forth.
